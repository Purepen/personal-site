JU & TOSZ Sales Dashboard

A Power BI sales dashboard that tracks product demand and regional performance across two years, built with a dark theme option, page navigation, and cool-toned bookmarks that make digging into the numbers feel good, not just informative.

Power BI Page navigation Theming

My second dashboard of that year, built on a sales product dataset spanning 2019 and 2020. The main KPIs are cities and sales, assessed across product categories and regions, with a Sales Overview page and a Cities Overview page linked by clean navigation so a viewer never has to guess where to click next. The MacBook Pro Laptop leads total sales at $8.0M, followed by iPhone and ThinkPad Laptop, while San Francisco, Los Angeles, and New York pull in the highest demand of any city in the dataset.

I leaned into the visual side here more than usual: a dark and light theme toggle right on the canvas, cool page transitions through bookmarks, and enough polish that the report holds up as something you'd actually want to present in a room, not just export as a screenshot.
