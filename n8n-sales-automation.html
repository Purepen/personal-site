AccountAI: an End-to-End AI Sales Pipeline

Drop in a PDF of who you're targeting, and a multi-agent n8n pipeline finds the prospects, drafts the outreach, and hands off to a voice agent that can take an inbound call and book the demo itself.

n8n ElevenLabs Twilio Pipedrive Firecrawl Hunter.io

I built a fully automated AI sales system that handles everything from finding the right prospects to booking a demo call with them, without me touching a CRM in the middle. It's two systems working together, one that finds and reaches out to people, and one that picks up the phone when they call back.

Outbound: the Business Development Manager

I drop a PDF describing my ideal customer profile into a Google Drive folder, and that's the whole trigger. An n8n AI agent reads the file, then hands off to two sub-agents: a prospecting tool that uses Firecrawl and Hunter.io over MCP to find matching companies and emails online, and an operations tool that saves each one into Pipedrive as an organized lead. A separate SDR sub-agent then drafts a personalized outreach email for every prospect it finds. I just review and send.

Inbound: a voice agent that closes the loop

The second half is a US phone number through Twilio, connected to an ElevenLabs voice agent. When a prospect calls in, the agent holds an actual conversation, and depending on what the caller needs, it can either record them as a deal directly in the CRM or check my Google Calendar for a mutual opening and book the demo right there on the call. Both systems talk to each other through n8n workflows and webhooks against a shared CRM, and every action fires a Pushover notification so I stay in the loop without babysitting any of it.

What was actually hard

None of this was a weekend build. The platforms powerful enough to do this properly sit behind real usage costs, getting multiple agents to return consistent structured output took a lot of prompt iteration, and routing the voice agent correctly between its tools needed serious testing before it stopped guessing. But watching a cold PDF turn into a booked demo call with no manual step in between made every hour of it worth it. This is what AI automation looks like once you go past chatbots and actually embed the intelligence into the workflow itself.
