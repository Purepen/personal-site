Credit Card Transaction Dashboard

A Power BI report breaking down Indian credit card transactions by city, expense category, and region, built to feel effortless to click through, not just accurate.

Power BI Bookmarks DAX

My first report of the year, built on an Indian credit card transaction dataset. The focus sits on three KPIs: which cities spend the most, how expenses break down, and how the totals shift once you slice by transaction category and region. Greater Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Ahmedabad lead the pack by total transaction amount, each clearing over half a billion in the dataset's currency units, while the smaller cities at the bottom of the table tell the opposite story: fewer withdrawals, but a higher amount per transaction, worth a second look on its own.

The part I cared most about here was the experience of using it, not just the numbers behind it. Bookmarks let a viewer jump between the cities view, the expenses view, and the amount view without the report ever feeling like separate pages bolted together, and the context tweaks underneath make sure a filter selected on one view carries its meaning correctly onto the next.
