Car Sales Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard built on a proper star-schema data model, with time-intelligence DAX, bookmarks, and page navigation, turning a raw car sales export into something a sales manager would actually enjoy clicking through.

Power BI DAX Power Query Data modeling

As one year folded into the next, I wanted a dashboard that didn't just show numbers but made a sales manager want to explore them. This one is built on a public car sales dataset from Saddam Ansari, and it covers total sales, profit growth, and the patterns hiding inside sales segments, transmission type, car color, and region.

A model built to be reused, not just filled in

Cleaning started in Excel, then moved into Power Query to enforce a clean one-to-many relationship against the Order table, which is the transactional fact table at the center of everything. Rather than one flat sheet, I split the data into seven purpose-built tables: Calendar, Customer, Dealer Details, Manager, Order, Product, and Returned Product, each connected back to the Order table by ID. It's more setup work up front, but it's the difference between a report that answers one question and a model that can answer whatever question comes up next.

DAX that does more than sum a column

The measures here go past basic totals into real time intelligence: current-period and prior-year profit using SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, a growth-and-gap composite that reports both the percentage and the dollar delta in one label, and color-coded up/down indicators driven by SWITCH logic so the chart tells you at a glance whether a category is beating or missing its target.

#PLY = var _blank = ISBLANK(SELECTEDVALUE('Calendar'[Year])) VAR _PreviousYear = MAX('Calendar'[Year]) - 1 VAR _FilteredPLY = CALCULATE([T.Profit], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Calendar'[Date])) RETURN if(_blank, CALCULATE([T.Profit], 'Calendar'[Year] = _PreviousYear), _FilteredPLY)

On top of the measures, the report leans on bookmarks and page navigation to let a viewer move between a sales overview, a profit-based view, and a details page without ever losing context, plus tooltips and an Android-style interface treatment to keep it visually distinct from the usual Power BI default.
